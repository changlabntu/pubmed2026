# Homework: Module 1

Open the app, run the steps from Q0 to Q5, and **download the report** from
the sidebar. The report is your submission. Before you download it, fill the
comment box under each step; empty comments mean an incomplete homework.

## Required run

0. Q0: keep the default question and gap for the first pass. Later, rewrite
   the gap in your own words and say what paper would disprove it.
1. Q1: use the pool your instructor assigned, or your own PubMed query if told so.
   Note the funnel line: how many records PubMed matched and how many you fetched.
2. Q2: keep the default question first. Grade. Then change **one thing** in
   the grading prompt (a score band's wording, a new return field, a stricter rule for 1-2)
   and grade again. Your comment says what you changed and what moved.
3. Q3: run **both strategies**: Embedding, then Anchor's neighbours. Your
   comment says which one found the paper you would have chosen, where the
   anchor ranked under Embedding, and whether the neighbours look like papers
   that answer the question or papers that merely resemble the anchor.
4. Q4: run once. Your comment says whether the synthesis narrowed the gap,
   conceded it, or kept it as written, and whether you agree. Then pick one
   `partial`, `insufficient` or `unsupported` row, read that abstract, and say
   whether you agree with the verifier.
5. Q5: run once. Your comment says whether you agree with the verdict, and
   names the single paper that comes closest to closing the gap.

## Reflection (in the Q4 comment, three sentences)

Which single change you made had the largest effect on the final review, and
how do you know?

## Rules

- Stay under the default 10 cent cap unless the instructor raises it.
- Haiku only, unless the instructor says otherwise.
- The report includes your prompts as edited. Do not edit the report by hand.
